# HPL Quotation Maker

Quotation generator for **Hayati Prospectors Limited** — gold detectors and mining equipment.
Branches: Lusaka (Lumumba Road, opposite Autoworld) and Kitwe (Nkana Mall, Shop G29).

**Open the app:** https://ahmedmoha79.github.io/hpl-quotation/

On iPhone: open the link in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.

- Build a quote on your phone, then tap **Download PDF** to share it on WhatsApp or email
- Manage products, prices and photos in the **Products** tab
- Every downloaded quote is kept in **History**
- Use **Settings → Export / Import catalogue** to give the whole team the same products and prices
